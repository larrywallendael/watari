How sync works on stage

Clock ownerThe laptop runs the story. The iPhone only renders and sends taps.
SoundRingtone + Watari voice + your mic all on the iPhone. Laptop silent until you hang up, then app sounds.
Your voiceiPhone mic → Soniox streaming STT → words stream to both screens. Watari waits for end of your sentence.
Watari voiceClaude picks the next line from the plan (scripted lines first, free answers when you deviate) → ElevenLabs TTS streamed on the iPhone. Phone sends the voice level so the big orb moves with it.
LinkOne WebSocket room (e.g. Ably / Pusher / PartyKit; Vercel functions can't hold sockets). Target <150 ms.
FallbackN plays your scripted line, Y approves, B backup video. Same visuals.

Before you go up

  • iPhone: Do Not Disturb on, Auto-Lock Never, brightness max, silent switch off
  • Open the phone page full screen (Add to Home Screen = no Safari bars)
  • Tap "arm audio" on both screens
  • Own hotspot, never the venue Wi-Fi
  • Mic close to the phone speaker
  • Backup video in a second tab, key B

iOS Safari can't vibrate from a web page. If the buzz matters, the build can place a real call (ElevenLabs + Twilio): native ring and vibration, but then the call screen is Apple's, not ours.

Before you pick

  • Real names: Colruyt is in as you asked, but only as a text mark. Showing a real Belgian company as a late payer, in front of KBC, can backfire. One line in W.CFG.client switches it to a fictional client.
  • Logos: partners are text marks. Drop official SVGs in core/logos/ and open with ?logos=1.
  • Voice in this prototype is the browser voice. ElevenLabs replaces it in the build.
